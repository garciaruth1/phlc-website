Home Safety Audits

A calm, thorough, room-by-room assessment that identifies hazards before they become emergencies.

Why It Matters

Independence is protected by preparation.

Most falls and household accidents happen in the rooms we know best — because it's easy to overlook a loose rug, a dim stairwell, or a bathroom without proper support. Our Home Safety Audit brings a trained, discreet eye to every room, so nothing is left to chance.

A consultant visits your home at a time that suits you, walks through each living space, and prepares a clear, practical report — no jargon, no alarmism, simply a plan for a safer home.

What We Assess

A thorough review of every living space.

Fall Hazards

Flooring, lighting, rugs, thresholds and stairways assessed for trip and slip risk.

Fire Risks

Smoke detectors, appliance placement and electrical safety reviewed room by room.

Accessibility

Doorways, bathrooms and stairs evaluated for ease of movement and support.

Everyday Dangers

Kitchen safety, medication storage and emergency accessibility considered.

The Process

From first visit to lasting peace of mind.

I

Private Walkthrough

A consultant tours your home and discusses your daily routines and any concerns.

II

Written Report

You receive a clear, prioritized report with practical, affordable recommendations.

III

Guided Follow-Through

If you'd like, we coordinate trusted vendors to implement any recommended changes.
