About Prestige Heritage

Trusted lifestyle support, built on a simple belief.

Our Mission

Everyday living, simplified with care.

Prestige Heritage Lifestyle Concierge was founded on the observation that the small logistics of daily life — the vendor who never calls back, the grocery run that never quite fits into the day, the appointment nobody can accompany you to — are rarely small at all. Left unattended, they quietly erode independence and peace of mind.

We built a concierge practice around trust rather than transactions. Every service we offer, from a room-by-room safety audit to a companion for a doctor's visit, is delivered by the same standard: discreet, dependable, and genuinely personal.

Meet the Founder

A legacy of service.
A commitment to excellence.

For more than 17 years, Ruth Garcia dedicated her career to serving and protecting others as a firefighter and emergency responder. Throughout that time, she witnessed firsthand how life's everyday challenges can become overwhelming for seniors and their families, particularly when safety, mobility, and reliable support are concerns.

That experience inspired the creation of Prestige Heritage Lifestyle Concierge — a premium concierge service built on trust, professionalism, compassion, and discretion.

Ruth believes that growing older should never mean sacrificing independence or quality of life. Every service is thoughtfully designed to help clients continue living comfortably, confidently, and safely in the place they call home. Whether coordinating appointments, conducting home safety assessments, managing everyday errands, or providing personalized lifestyle support, every interaction reflects the highest standards of care and respect.

Her background in emergency services provides a unique perspective on prevention, preparedness, and client advocacy — allowing Prestige Heritage Lifestyle Concierge to deliver proactive solutions rather than simply responding to problems.

Today, Ruth leads the company with one clear mission: to provide exceptional concierge services that preserve dignity, promote independence, and give families complete peace of mind.
